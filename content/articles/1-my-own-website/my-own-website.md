## Hi

I've finally built my own portfolio website that features my projects that I did independantly or the projects I did during my university period. I also included an articles section to write blog posts such as this once in a while. 

It is refreshing to have your own personal website because at least you know the things you put in there don't get suddenly **deleted or removed** for whatever reason. You own that website and no one have any **authority** over it. Excluding the hosting services that the website is hosted in.

## What Else?

Maybe I will change the design a bit to make the projects or articles stand out in the front. Other than that I will keep this website simple as possible.

## Why Is The Website Static?

It's much easier to host websites that are static and besides, I don't want to setup a web service just for a simple portfolio website.
